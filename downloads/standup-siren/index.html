Standup Siren

A tiny local tray app that plays dramatic music shortly before your daily team meeting. No backend. No account. No cloud sludge. Just a small goblin with very strong editorial instincts.

Download for macOS Download for Linux

Default meeting time is 13:00. Power users can override the sound with their own ring.mp3 in the config directory.

What it does

Standup Siren sits quietly in your tray and starts the soundtrack shortly before your standup. The point is not productivity theatre. The point is that meetings deserve at least a little cinema.

Why it exists

Because a tiny local utility with one funny, useful job is better than yet another dashboard-shaped life problem.
